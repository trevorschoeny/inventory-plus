Requires MenuKit 6.2.0 or newer, below 7.0.0, and Keybindery 1.1.0 or newer. Your locks, keys and other settings carry over when you update.

The tooltip setting that kept long tooltips out of the way now docks them instead, and is called Dock tooltips at top or bottom. It starts off, even if you had the old setting on; turn it on in the Tooltips tab of the settings menu. While it is on, a tooltip that would open beside your mouse sits at the bottom centre of the screen, or at the top centre when your mouse is down there.

The Inventory Plus section of the Controls screen has its name back. It was showing a code like key.category.inventoryplus.controls instead.
