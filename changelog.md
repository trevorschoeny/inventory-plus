Requires MenuKit 6.2.0 or newer, below 7.0.0, and Keybindery 1.1.0 or newer. Your locks, settings and keys carry over when you update.

Now runs on Minecraft 26.3.

The tooltip setting that kept long tooltips out of the way now docks them instead, and is called Dock tooltips at top or bottom. It is off by default; turn it on in the Tooltips tab of the settings menu. While it is on, a tooltip that would open beside your mouse sits at the bottom centre of the screen, or at the top centre when your mouse is down there.
