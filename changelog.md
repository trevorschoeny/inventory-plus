Hotbar Cycler is new, under Power Users and off by default. Hover an inventory row and click the small button beside it to add that row to the cycle. Press ] to bring the next row down into your hotbar and [ to send it back up. Items stay in their own columns, and the hotbar slides to show the change. Sorting and automation leave rows in the cycle alone.

Column Cycler now rotates with the Up and Down arrow keys. The brackets belong to Hotbar Cycler. If you already had Column Cycler on the brackets, your saved binding stays and both cyclers will answer to the brackets until you rebind one under Controls.

Column Cycler's overlay beside the hotbar is now always a vertical column.

Sort keeps every stack of an item together, with the items you have most of first.
