## 3.3.0

Requires MenuKit 7.4.0 or newer and SlotKit 2.1.0 or newer.

### Fixed

- Locks and Reach now hold in creative, on the inventory tab and on item tabs, as they do in survival.
- The hotbar's slide when Hotbar Cycler turns now ends when the strip's does.

### Changed

- The feature buttons on the right now show on creative's inventory tab too. They stay off item tabs, which have no inventory.
- Sort works on creative's inventory tab, with its button and its key.
- Column Cycler's keys turn a column in creative's inventory too, on the inventory tab and, with the mouse on a hotbar slot, on item tabs.

## 3.2.0

### Added

- Restock can take from shulker boxes and bundles in your inventory, and from your ender chest, when your inventory has nothing that fits. It's set on the Reach tab for items, tools, armor, and ammo, and each starts off.
- Auto Tool Switch can take a tool or weapon from a shulker box or bundle in your inventory. It's set on the Reach tab and starts off.
- Pick block can take the picked block's item from a shulker box, a bundle, or your ender chest when your inventory has none. It's set on the Reach tab and starts off.

### Changed

- Lock groups are a list on their tab. Add, delete, and reorder groups with the buttons above it. The order is the order the Lock groups switch goes through.
- A group's settings stay open when you rename it, recolor it, or add another group.
- A group's color is picked from the 16 dye colors or any color on the Custom tab.
- Item and Exact item groups list the items they lock, and you can add items from a searchable grid.
- The Look setting names the look in use, and the Options screen look is now called Default instead of Classic.
- Requires MenuKit 7.3.0 or newer, below 8.0.0, and SlotKit 2.0.0 or newer, below 3.0.0.
- It still works on any server, vanilla included, with nothing installed there. Your locks, keys, and other settings carry over when you update.
