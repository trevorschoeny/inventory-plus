## 3.2.0

### Added

- Restock can take from shulker boxes and bundles in your inventory, and from your ender chest, when your inventory has nothing that fits. It's set on the Reach tab for items, tools, armor, and ammo, and each starts off.
- Auto Tool Switch can take a tool or weapon from a shulker box or bundle in your inventory. It's set on the Reach tab and starts off.
- Pick block can take the picked block's item from a shulker box, a bundle, or your ender chest when your inventory has none. It's set on the Reach tab and starts off.

### Changed

- The General tab's Look setting is now a toggle, not a button you click to cycle. It reads Look: Default or Look: Panel for the look in use; clicking it switches to the other one.
- The option screen look is now called Default; it was Classic.
- Lock groups are a list now. Pick a group, then delete it or move it up or down with the buttons above the list. The order is the order the lock button cycles through. The two default groups stay at the top. Deleting a group asks first, saying where its locks go.
- A group's settings stay open when you rename it, recolor it, or add another group.
- A group's color is a color picker now: the 16 dye colors, or any color you like on its Custom tab.
- Item and exact item groups list their items in their settings. Click the empty slot to pick items from a searchable grid of every item, or remove one with its corner button. Locks are kept per world, so pick them while you're in one.
- Requires MenuKit 7.3.0 or newer, below 8.0.0, and SlotKit 2.0.0 or newer, below 3.0.0.
- It still works on any server, vanilla included, with nothing installed there. Your locks, keys, and other settings carry over when you update.

## 3.1.0

### Changed

- When a screen opens, tooltips stay hidden until you move the mouse, so no tooltip appears under the cursor the moment a chest or your inventory opens. This works on every screen, not only your inventory.
- This replaces the 3.0.0 change that moved the cursor above the slots when your inventory opened, which could move the cursor even while the game window was in the background. The cursor is no longer moved.
- The setting is Hide tooltips until the mouse moves, on the Tooltips tab, and it's on by default. If you had turned off the old cursor setting, the new one starts on, so turn it off again if you don't want it.
- Requires MenuKit 7.2.0 or newer, below 8.0.0, and SlotKit 1.1.0 or newer, below 2.0.0.
- It still works on any server, vanilla included, with nothing installed there. Your locks, keys, and other settings carry over when you update.
