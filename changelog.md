Requires MenuKit 6.1.0 or newer, below 7.0.0, and Keybindery 1.1.0 or newer. Your locks, settings and keys carry over when you update.

The settings menu now looks like Minecraft's own Options screens, with the world blurred behind it, the tabs down the left and Done at the bottom. If you prefer the old look, the Look button on the General tab switches between Classic and Panel.

Every tab now opens with its title and description, then Reset to Defaults and the feature's On and Off switch. When a feature is off, everything on its tab greys out. Tooltips has moved up to sit right under General.

The buttons around your inventory are reorganised. The row above it is for doing things: Move Matching out, Move Matching in, and Sort. The column on the right turns features on and off, with the gear at the top and a switch for each feature below it. Left-click a switch to turn its feature on or off, and right-click the Lock groups switch to pick the group L locks with. Ctrl-click (Cmd on a Mac) any button to open its tab, and its tooltip says so while you hold Ctrl. If the column reaches the bottom of your inventory, it carries on in a second column.

The Column Cycler and Hotbar Cycler switches start hidden. To show one, tick Show the Column Cycler button or Show the Hotbar Cycler button on that feature's tab. If you had the Column Cycler button showing before, its switch stays showing.

Column Cycler and the lock button no longer have edit modes. Press C over a slot to add it to a column, and L over a slot or an item to lock it.

Sort and Move Matching now say in one line what a click will do, such as "Sort by Category", with right-click to change it on the line below. You can pin a mode to one container only where that means something, which is the Sort button above a container and Move Matching out. With only your inventory open, the Sort key now sorts it wherever your mouse is.

Auto Tool Switch can now switch back on its own once you stop, after a wait you set from 0 to 10 seconds, and you can add a key that switches back early. New installs start with switching back on, after one second. If you had switching back off, it stays off.

A new Compact tooltips setting on General shortens every inventory button's tooltip to one line.

On the Tooltips tab, Keep long tooltips out of the way opens a tall tooltip on whichever side covers less of your inventory. Hold a key to hide tooltips is in the same section. Scroll long tooltips and Keep tooltips on screen are no longer settings, and both are always on while Tooltips is on.

When you cycle with Hotbar Cycler while Inventory Max's pocket strip is showing, the held item in the strip now slides up or down with the hotbar instead of the whole strip sliding sideways.

On a new install, Column Cycler's Up key cycles forward and Down cycles back. An existing install keeps the keys it has. To swap them, set Column Cycler's forward and backward keys in Controls.

Fixed: Column Cycler stopped cycling partway when two stacks of the same item met in a column, and cycling could make Restock move your hand to another hotbar slot.
